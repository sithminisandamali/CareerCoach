Overview of the Sri Lankan IT Job Market for New Graduates

The Sri Lankan IT/BPM sector is one of the country's largest export industries, with strong demand for software engineers, QA engineers, and business analysts, particularly from companies serving UK, US, and Australian clients.

Common technology stacks requested in local job postings include MERN/MEAN stack, Java/Spring Boot, .NET, and increasingly React Native/Flutter for mobile. Many mid-size local companies also request SQL and basic DevOps familiarity (Docker, CI/CD) even for junior roles.

Entry-level interviews at Sri Lankan IT companies commonly include: a technical screening call, a take-home coding assignment or live coding round, and a final HR/culture-fit round. Behavioral questions frequently probe teamwork under deadline pressure, given the prevalence of agile delivery to overseas clients across time zones.

Salary expectations for fresh graduates vary significantly between BOI-registered exporters, local product companies, and small agencies; researching Glassdoor/LinkedIn ranges for the specific company type before an offer negotiation is recommended.
