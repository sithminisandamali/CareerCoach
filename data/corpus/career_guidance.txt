General Career Guidance Notes for BSc (Hons) IT Undergraduates

Final-year students should aim to have at least 2-3 substantial full-stack or specialized projects (e.g. one MERN/MEAN project, one mobile app, one project in a secondary stack like Spring Boot) documented with clear READMEs before applying for graduate roles.

Building a portfolio site or GitHub Pages presence alongside the CV helps differentiate candidates, especially when it links to live demos of deployed projects.

Networking through university career fairs, LinkedIn, and alumni referrals remains one of the highest-yield channels for securing interviews at Sri Lankan tech companies, alongside direct applications.

Soft skills consistently cited by local employers as differentiators include clear written communication (important for remote/distributed teams) and the ability to explain technical decisions to non-technical stakeholders.
